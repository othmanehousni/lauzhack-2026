The fresh air helps me think clearly before a busy day begins.
I usually drink a cup of black coffee with a slice of warm toast.
Then I check my phone for messages from my family and friends.
My brother lives far away, so we often share photos and videos.

Her name is Ruby, and she chews everything she can find, even my shoes.

We walk for hours, talk about our jobs, and laugh about old memories.
Thursday is usually my busiest day, with meetings from nine until five.
I try to stay calm, speak clearly, and answer every question honestly.
When the weather is bad, I stay home and enjoy a good book.
My favorite stories are about brave people who change the world.
At night, I cook something simple like pasta, soup, or vegetables.
Before bed, I write a short list of things I want to do tomorrow.
Small habits like these keep my life balanced, healthy, and happy.
