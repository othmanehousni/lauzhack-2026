The Mole.

A companion for meetings. While you talk, it finds the slide or file that backs your point and puts it on the shared screen. It presents from a VM of its own, so your desktop and the call stay yours, and it follows you to your phone when you walk.

The desktop app starts a VM and shares its window, Copilot screen, in the call. The agent presents there and never touches your own desktop. The mole sits in a bar over your desktop and says what it understood.

On the phone

It follows you when you walk. Stand up and walk the room. Past three metres from the laptop, the phone's microphone takes the lead, so the mole still hears you and keeps the screen in step with the talk.

0.4 m

When it hesitates, it asks. If two slides or files fit what you said, the phone buzzes and offers them. Tap one or say its number. Do nothing and the screen stays as it is.

    Under the hood

    One codebase. Kotlin Multiplatform shares the agent, built on Koog, between the desktop app and the Android companion.

    Nothing leaves the room. EmbeddingGemma embeds each sentence on the device, and a Gemma model fine-tuned on meeting talk decides what to show.

    A stage of its own. The VM holds the decks, files and browser the agent drives. Your desktop stays as you left it.

    Twice a second, a coroutine asks the agent whether there is anything to do. It acted. It asked you.

    // commonMain: the same loop runs on the laptop and on the phone
flow {
    while (true) {
        emit(agent.anythingToDo(heard.value, onScreen.value))
        delay(500.milliseconds)
    }
}.collect { move ->
    when (move) {
        is Move.Show -> vm.show(move.target)
        is Move.Ask -> phone.buzz().ask(move.options)
        Move.Nothing -> Unit
    }
}
