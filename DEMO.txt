MOLIÈRE: READ THIS BEFORE GOING ON STAGE


1. LAUNCH

  Quit any open Molière first (menu bar mole, Quit). Then:

    open ~/Desktop/lauzhack-2026/composeApp/build/compose/binaries/main/app/Moliere.app --args --library ~/Desktop/lauzhack-2026/pitch --no-history

  The shared screen is the window "Molière screen". It has no Dock icon: find it with Mission Control
  (Control and Up arrow). In the call, share that window, then click "Start a meeting".

3. WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  A wrong thing is announced:     say "no" while it announces (or press Stop on the bar or the phone).
  A wrong screen is shown:        say "no, go back" within 20 seconds.
  Nothing happens:                say it again, slower.
  The microphone fails:           quit Molière and type the lines instead, Return after each one,
                                  once it says "Hi. I'm listening.":
    ~/Desktop/lauzhack-2026/composeApp/build/compose/binaries/main/app/Moliere.app/Contents/MacOS/Moliere --typed --library ~/Desktop/lauzhack-2026/pitch --no-history


4. WORDING TO AVOID

  "Close that." after a diagram: it goes to the desktop, not the deck. Say "Go back to slide 8." instead.
  Statements as picks ("We asked six people...", "The real problem is...") are taken as talk.
  "Show me the slide on tech failures" picked slide 1.


5. MIME MODE

  Once:    brew install git-lfs && git lfs pull
           lipreading/setup.sh
  In the app: end the meeting, open Settings, set Mode to Mime, press Start miming.
           The bar shows the mime mole and the words as they are read.
  Alone:   cd lipreading && .venv/bin/python lipread.py
  Keys:    in the lip reader's camera window: SPACE pauses and resumes,
           V turns the voice off, Q quits.
  Memory:  the lip reader loads about 6 GB. End the meeting before starting it.
