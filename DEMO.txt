2. THE REAL THING, WITH YOUR VOICE

  a. Open the app:   open composeApp/build/compose/binaries/main/app/Moliere.app
     There is no Dock icon. Look for the mole in the menu bar.
  b. Decks: the app reads PDFs from ~/Documents/Moliere. For the demo either copy your decks there,
     or menu bar mole, "Choose decks folder...", and pick the repo's library folder.
  c. Menu bar mole, "Start a meeting". The first time, macOS asks for: Microphone, Speech Recognition,
     Local Network (the link to the VM and the phone), and Calendar on the first real call. Allow each.
     If you missed one: System Settings, Privacy & Security, enable the app under that heading, then
     "End the meeting" and "Start a meeting" again.
     NOT VERIFIED BY A PERSON: nobody was awake to click these prompts. Do this once before the demo,
     and do it AFTER the last rebuild (a rebuild is a new signature, macOS may ask again).
  d. In the call (Meet, Zoom, Teams), share the VM window. It is titled "Copilot screen" until the VM is
     next restarted, then "Molière screen".
  e. Speak the lines of section 1. With a real call the meeting starts by itself about 5 seconds after the
     call app takes the microphone (NOT VERIFIED BY A PERSON); "Start a meeting" always works.

  What was verified without a person: the packaged app was fed synthesized speech files through Apple's
  recognizer (no microphone, no sound) and acted on them correctly. Measured from the end of the voice:
  a command reaches the shared screen in 1.5 to 1.8 seconds, an announce appears after about 2 seconds.
  An agent is working on bringing both under about one second; see the end of this file for the result.


3. SAY NO OR IT GETS SHOWN

  Anything the mole picks by meaning is announced first: the bar says "Showing ..." with a Stop button
  that drains for 2.5 seconds. Three ways to stop it:
    say "no" (or "no, not that one") before the Stop runs out     tested in the real app
    tap Stop on the phone                                         tested on an emulator against the real app
    click Stop on the bar                                         tested in code only, nobody clicked it
  Ask again after a stop and it is announced again.
  After it is shown, "no, go back" within 20 seconds restores the previous screen (tested at 13 seconds).
  When what you talk about is simply the next slide of the deck on screen, it moves on at once, like a click.
  Commands on what is already on screen (next, previous, slide 7, scroll, close) act at once.
  Menu bar mole, "Wait before showing": 2.5 s, 1.5 s, or none. Try all three and keep the one that feels right.
  NOT VERIFIED BY A PERSON: clicking the tray menu items (they are built and the app runs; nobody clicked).

6. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG DURING THE DEMO

  The bar says "Couldn't get the shared screen ready."   The app starts the VM by itself; if that failed:  agent-desktop sandbox start base --window
  Nothing happens when you speak.                         Menu bar mole, "End the meeting", "Start a meeting". Check the microphone permission.
  It picks the wrong thing.                               Say "no". That is the feature.
  You want the old behaviour.                             Start with --rules (the hand-written rules, slower to start, no "no").
  No VM at all.                                           Start with --host (a Chrome window on this Mac is the shared screen).
  Look at what it heard and decided.                      ~/Library/Logs/Moliere/moliere.log (every word, decision and screen call, timestamped)

  Other ways to start:
    --demo         the scripted story on the bar, no models, no VM
    --playground   every state of the mole one click away, light and dark
    --paced        with --typed: each line arrives word by word, like speech
    --audio DIR    hears audio files instead of the microphone
    --window MS    the wait before showing, for one run
    --models DIR   another folder of models for one run (to try a new decider without replacing the installed one)
